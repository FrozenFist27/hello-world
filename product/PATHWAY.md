# Seven looks, one hand: the learning pathway for Hold On

*The coach stops your hand for seven looks; each one you go whole games without needing, it asks about with fewer words, then not at all, and the first time a piece goes anyway it starts asking again.*

The iteration spec the team converged on (seven partners, cross-review, synthesis, four critics, one revision). It is the build contract for this iteration; `product/BRIEF-2.md` is the brief and `product/ideation-2.json` the full record.

## Thesis

Where we are first, since that was the question: the Hold On MVP is built, published and green today (2195 gate checks, 285 validator checks, copy_check over 111 templates, the headless harness passing at 1280 and 400 px in both themes in 221 s stub / 174 s no-stub); iteration two exists as a committed brief (611fd60, product/BRIEF-2.md) and as this spec, which lives only in the workflow until it is committed; none of its ten modules has a line of code; the chess study side has not moved since 1 October and holds no games, so every mastery rule here is designed for a hold log that does not exist yet. The next milestone is a committed contract (product/PRODUCT-2.md plus the review record) and a green path.js over synthetic logs. The design itself survives the critiques with its spine intact: progress is the coach's hand withdrawing one look at a time, with the gate still judging after the hand stops showing, so silence is measured and not assumed. What the critiques corrected, and this revision accepts, is the arithmetic under that spine: a capture that lands where a pawn takes it is the pawns look (Nxe5 stays the tutorial's own lesson); counted games include quick decisive ones; slips count wherever they happen; the floors read a fixed trailing window rather than a run that resets; the fold runs game by game so a prerequisite is judged on the stage it had then; the ladder folds over an append-only digest so trimming and merging cannot rewrite history; 'yours' is said only after five clean counted games of a look that has actually fired, and a look that never fires caps at the bare hand; both rewards are recorded; the K3 question is four sentences; the mirror never writes before it reads and never prompts over a board; the harness stubs the mirror and gets an honest time budget. The gift order leaves the iteration (the notch alone follows the free look), which removes the only change to the measured opponent. Everything else stays: one page, seven serif sentences in three inks with one amber dot and no numeral, one quick-tier call per game after consent, every field validated against an empty square set with a template underneath.

## The model

KIND. A ladder of seven looks in a fixed order, each named as a result in the player's words, each mapped one-to-one onto a gate category, sub-case or split the record already carries. A ladder and not a map because the current rung moves only by rule. The order (pawns, takers, takes_back, their_last_move, free, guards, mates) is a deliberate departure from the chess master's lesson order, which puts their-last-move first: the gate's data says the pawn landing is the commonest and cheapest event, so it is placed first and stated as such. NODES: pawns, takers, takes_back, their_last_move, free, guards, mates. nodeOf(record) is a pure function with pawnAmongTakers as the FIRST split: hanging_after_move with pawnAmongTakers true is pawns whether or not the move captured (Nxe5 in the 6...d6 position, where dxe5 answers, is pawns); hanging_after_move with pawnAmongTakers false and variant 'takes_back' is takes_back (Bxf7+ with the king taking back); hanging_after_move with pawnAmongTakers false and variant null is takers; ignored_attack sub 'already' is their_last_move; sub 'lost_guard' or 'opened_line' is guards; free_piece_ignored is free; allowed_mate, missed_mate and allowed_stalemate are mates. For a v1 record the split is derived by chess.js (the pawn attackers of record.to in record.fen after record.san, and 'x' in san), and the expected look for every recorded move in data/positions.json and tutorial.json is written as an explicit table in test_path.mjs (italian_d6 Nxe5 pawns, Bxf7+ takes_back, queen_hang Qxe5+ and Qxf7+ takes_back, tutorial Nd4/Ba6/Be6 pawns, ruy_a6 O-O their_last_move, nc4_rook Nc4 takers, italian_nxe4 Nc3 guards, f3_e5 g4 mates, scholars Qh3 mates, kq_stalemate Qf7 mates). A RECORD of a look is any HoldRecord with nodeOf(record) === look and outcome back, anyway, ended or landed (a verdict the gate matched but did not show, why 'pair', 'quiet' or 'hand'), in any game that is not an 'again' game; tutorial holds are excluded. OBSERVED is the count of committed player moves with a non-null verdict (taken-back moves and cold-engine moves excluded). A COUNTED game is a digest with again false and (observed >= PATH.OBSERVED_MIN (20) or (result in {mate-win, mate-loss, resign} and observed >= PATH.OBSERVED_DECISIVE_MIN (8))); the plies clause is gone (it was implied). Give-ups and mercy under those floors count as clean evidence for nothing, but their records still count against (next paragraph). HAND STATES per look: asking (the hold fires with its category question), quiet (the hold fires, the piece lifts, the square breathes amber, the board dims, every tap is graded as today, but the line says only 'Hold on. You know this one. Tap it.'; the hint after a wrong tap and the naming after a second are unchanged), off (the hold does not open: gate.verdict returns suppressed 'hand' with the full facts, the move lands, the opponent takes as it always does, the record is logged 'landed' why 'hand', givenAway counts it, the close card captions it 'Knight to e5, a pawn was waiting: it landed.'). ALWAYS_HOLD (the mates look) and any verdict with netLoss >= GATE.BIG_LOSS (5) ignore the hand in every state. THE DIGEST. At storeGame every game appends one permanent digest line to holdon.v1.path.digest: { id, date, result, observed, again, captures, escapes, gifts: {offered, taken}, looks: { lookId: { n, w, netLoss } } } (n records, w their summed weight), about 120 bytes, kept to PATH.DIGEST_MAX (200) newest, never trimmed with the summaries. compute(digest, now) runs over the digest, so trimming the twelve full summaries and merging another device's games can never rewrite a stage he was shown. THE FOLD, game-major: for each digest line oldest to newest, for each look in ladder order, update state {stage: 'asking'|'quiet'|'off', run, woken, everFired}. Weight of a record: 1 when outcome is back and the first graded tap (taps[0]) was right or partial on whatever look it was graded on (a right tap on a K2 ask is not punished), else 2 (anyway, ended, landed, a back after a named or wrong first tap). DECAY runs over every non-again game, counted or not: at off, one record of any weight sends the look to quiet with woken true (the slip is named, the bare hold returns); two or more records in one game send it to asking; at quiet, any weight-2 record or two or more records send it to asking, a single weight-1 record keeps quiet; at asking nothing changes; run = 0 in every case; everFired = true. CLIMB runs only over counted games with zero records of the look: run += 1; asking -> quiet when run >= PATH.CLEAN_TO_QUIET (2) and the floor holds and the prerequisite (its stage after its own update for this same game) is at quiet or off, then run = 0; quiet -> off when run >= (woken ? PATH.WOKEN_TO_OFF (2) : PATH.QUIET_TO_OFF (1)) and the floor holds and the last PATH.OFF_WINDOW (5) counted games hold zero records of the look and (everFired or the look's positive evidence exists: gifts taken for free, escapes for their_last_move), then run = 0 and woken = false. A look with no record ever and no positive evidence therefore caps at quiet and is never announced as 'yours'. FLOORS are opportunity conditions read over a fixed trailing window of the last PATH.FLOOR_WINDOW (3) counted games including the current one, at every climb, independent of the run: pawns and takers none beyond the counted floor; takes_back captures summed >= PATH.CAPTURES_MIN (3); their_last_move escapes summed >= PATH.ESCAPES_MIN (2) or everFired; free gifts.offered summed >= PATH.GIFTS_MIN (2) and gifts.taken >= gifts.offered - 1; guards the digest holds at least PATH.GUARDS_HISTORY (5) counted games in all. PREREQUISITES gate the climb, never the measurement: takers needs pawns at quiet or off; takes_back needs takers; guards needs their_last_move; a gated look keeps counting its run and climbs in the same game its prerequisite does, judged against the prerequisite's stage in that game, not its final one. MATES: the hand never fades; words only: 'back' when the newest stored game of any kind holds a record of the kind; 'quiet' when the last PATH.MATE_WINDOW (5) counted games hold zero records and a record exists earlier in the digest; else 'asking'; a mate-win is not evidence either way. IDLE: when now minus the newest digest line's date (any kind) >= PATH.IDLE_DAYS (14), every off look reads quiet for the next game (plan.review true; 'It has been a while. I will hold a little more today.'); the ordinary fold restores or wakes it. NOT-YET on the face: a look with everFired false and fewer than three counted games collapses into one muted line; its hand is asking. CURRENT: the first look in ladder order at asking (mates excluded); when pawns and free are both at asking, the one with more records in the last three counted games, then the larger netLoss3 (material at stake), ties to pawns; if no look is at asking, the first at quiet; if every material look is off or capped quiet with everFired false, current is null and the next game opens 'There is nothing left for me to hold. Your move.' TRANSITIONS are a diff against holdon.v1.path.shown, the stages at the last time a line was said, written at newGame and endGame, and computed only when the newest digest line is the game this view just played; after a merge, shown is reset silently to the merged stages, so an OFF, SLIP or WAKE sentence is said only for a change his own last game caused. NEXT, in one sentence: the next game is always a real game from the start position, played with each look's hand at its stage, the gift notch following free, the opening line and the remembering question following current; the only other thing the model offers is the player's own worst moment of the current look as a link on the close card, never on load.

## The looks (nodes)

### `pawns`: I see the pawn before my piece lands.

The look from their chair at the landing square, for the cheapest taker: before a knight, bishop, rook or queen lands, which enemy pawn reaches that square, whether or not the landing was itself a capture. The commonest way a game is lost under 1000 and the tutorial's own lesson (Nxe5 in the 6...d6 position, dxe5 answers). hanging_after_move with pawnAmongTakers true. Current by default on an empty log.

**Evidence.**
- Against: every HoldRecord with category hanging_after_move and pawnAmongTakers true (any variant), outcome back (weight 1 when taps[0] was right or partial on whatever look it was graded on, else 2), anyway, ended or landed (weight 2), in any non-again game counted or not. v1 records: a pawn attacks record.to in record.fen after record.san per chess.js.
- For: a counted game (observed >= 20, or a decisive result with observed >= 8; again false) with zero records of this kind. Punish-always makes every landing a test, so no further floor; the five-game off window and the one-record-ever rule carry the evidence for 'yours'.
- Copy only, never the stage: the first tap's grade (taps[].ask kept) feeds the K3 facts and the close caption.
- Ignored: tutorial holds, records in again games, cold-engine moves (not observed).

**Mastery rule.** asking -> quiet after two consecutive clean counted games (PATH.CLEAN_TO_QUIET 2); quiet -> off after one further clean counted game (QUIET_TO_OFF 1; WOKEN_TO_OFF 2 when woken) AND zero records in the last PATH.OFF_WINDOW (5) counted games AND at least one pawns record ever in the digest. Fixtures: one game with a pawns record then five clean counted games (observed 22-30) -> off at the sixth game, quiet at the third; the same with the clean games at observed 12 -> asking; five clean counted games with no pawns record ever -> quiet, never off, no OFF transition.

**Prerequisites.** none

**How it is practised.** While current: the next game's opening line names it with the countdown in words ('Still on the pawns. One more clean game and I ask with fewer words.'); a hold of this kind asks the remembering question at 0 ms when the newest stored game (counted or not) holds a record of this kind ('Hold on. The pawns again. Which one reaches that square? Tap it.', K3's validated question or COPY.PATH.QUESTION.pawns); the K2 prompt names it so a chosen ask leans toward what_takes_it or safe_square. The opponent is unchanged (the gift order is on the later list). At quiet: the hold fires with 'Hold on. You know this one. Tap it.'; Show me stays available. At off: no hold; the piece lands, the opponent takes, the close caption reads 'it landed'; the first off game opens 'I will not ask about the pawns today. They are yours.' (said once; holdon.v1.path.said).

**Retire or decay.** off -> quiet (woken) on one record in any non-again game, counted or not (a knight hung at move eight of a game given up at move twelve wakes the hand), the next game opening 'The pawns, once, and I said nothing. I will ask again.'; off -> asking on two records in one game; quiet -> asking on any weight-2 record or two records in one game, the next game opening 'The pawns again. I am holding those once more.'; a woken look needs two clean counted games, not one, to re-quiet, and then five clean counted games in the off window to go off again. Fourteen days without a stored game makes off read quiet for one game. Nothing retires for good.

### `takers`: I see what takes my piece before it lands.

The same look when the taker is a knight, bishop, rook, queen or king and the move was not a capture: the line is longer and the counting of defenders starts to matter. hanging_after_move with pawnAmongTakers false and variant null (nc4_rook Nc4).

**Evidence.**
- Against: HoldRecords with category hanging_after_move, pawnAmongTakers false, variant null, any outcome including landed, weighted as pawns.
- For: a counted game with zero records of this kind; off needs the five-game window and one record ever, as pawns.
- Copy only: first-tap grades.

**Mastery rule.** The same fold and constants as pawns, climbing only while pawns is at quiet or off in the same game (judged on pawns' stage after its own update for that game, game-major). Fixture: pawns and takers both fire in game 1 and are clean in games 2-7 -> takers quiet in the game pawns goes quiet and off in the game pawns goes off; a pawns weight-2 record appended as game 8 drops pawns to asking and leaves takers' stage and the earlier transitions untouched.

**Prerequisites.** pawns

**How it is practised.** While current: the opening line 'What takes it. Before the piece lands, look from their chair.'; the remembering question 'Hold on. Not a pawn this time. What reaches that square? Tap it.'. Quiet and off as pawns, with the look's own words in the announcements. No opponent change.

**Retire or decay.** As pawns.

### `takes_back`: Before I take, I see what takes back.

The capture that loses to the recapture when no pawn is among the takers: a bishop takes on f7 and the king takes back (the tutorial's own first hold), a queen takes a defended pawn and a piece takes her. hanging_after_move with pawnAmongTakers false and variant 'takes_back'. Placed after the piece look because a player who still hangs pieces to quiet moves is not ready to count exchanges.

**Evidence.**
- Against: HoldRecords with category hanging_after_move, pawnAmongTakers false, variant 'takes_back' (v1: 'x' in san and no pawn attacker), any outcome including landed, weighted as pawns.
- For: a counted game with zero records of this kind.
- Floor: captures summed over the last three counted games >= PATH.CAPTURES_MIN (3), from the per-game count of his applied moves that captured (a v1 game reads null and cannot satisfy it), read at every climb independently of the run.

**Mastery rule.** The same fold as pawns with the captures floor, climbing only while takers is at quiet or off. Fixtures: one game with a record, then five clean counted games with captures [1,1,1,1,1] -> off at the sixth; captures [0,0,0,0,0] -> asking; captures [2,2,1,1,1] -> off.

**Prerequisites.** takers

**How it is practised.** While current: the opening line 'Before you take, what takes back.'; the remembering question 'Hold on. This one again. Before you take there: what takes back? Tap it.'; the Show me ghost (the capture and the recapture) is the practice. No opponent change. Quiet and off as pawns.

**Retire or decay.** As pawns.

### `their_last_move`: I answer what their last move pointed at.

The chess master's first look (lesson 0001): what did their last move attack, and is it answered. The look that castles into a lost bishop in the Ruy after 3...a6. ignored_attack with sub 'already'. Its place fourth on the ladder, with no prerequisite, is a deliberate departure from the curriculum order, because the gate's data shows the pawn landing is the commoner and cheaper event.

**Evidence.**
- Against: HoldRecords with category ignored_attack and sub 'already', any outcome including landed, weighted as pawns (a partial tap on the attacker counts as right for the weight).
- For: a counted game with zero records of this kind, plus escapes: the escaped reward (a non-pawn piece of his worth >= 2 attacked by the opponent's last move before his move, nothing of his hanging after, cpLoss < 200) recorded per game whether or not it was said, and produced beside free_taken on the same move rather than instead of it.
- Floor: escapes summed over the last three counted games >= PATH.ESCAPES_MIN (2), or everFired; three clean games in which the opponent never attacked anything stay at asking and the face reads 'This is where we are.' without a count.

**Mastery rule.** The same fold as pawns with the escapes floor; no prerequisite. Fixtures: five clean counted games with escapes [1,1,0,1,1] and one earlier record -> off at the sixth; escapes [0,0,0,0,0] with no prior record -> asking.

**Prerequisites.** none

**How it is practised.** While current: the opening line 'Before your move, their last move. What did it touch?'; the remembering question 'Hold on. Their last move pointed at something of yours. Tap it.'; the 'You looked' reward wins the quiet budget over 'Free knight' when both apply in one move while this look is current (both are recorded either way); the K2 prompt names it so a chosen ask leans toward attacked_piece. The opponent is unchanged (threat shaping is on the later list). Quiet and off as pawns.

**Retire or decay.** As pawns.

### `free`: I take what is free.

A piece of theirs that nothing guards, walked past, including the opponent's own verified gifts. free_piece_ignored on the way down, the 'Free knight. You looked.' reward and the gift taken on the way up. The one look the opponent manufactures at a measured rate, and the look that owns the gift notch.

**Evidence.**
- Against: HoldRecords with category free_piece_ignored, any outcome including landed (a landed record of this kind loses nothing of his and never counts in givenAway), weighted as pawns. A walked-past verified gift is already measured by the gate as free_piece_ignored held or landed, so no synthetic record is added for it.
- For: rewards[] of kind free_taken and gifts.taken per game (offered when state().lastGift.verified, taken when his capture lands on gift.square), and a counted game with zero records of this kind; gifts taken count as positive evidence for the one-record-ever rule.
- Floor: gifts.offered summed over the last three counted games >= PATH.GIFTS_MIN (2) and gifts.taken >= gifts.offered - 1, reachable at the measured 1.5-1.85 gifts per game.

**Mastery rule.** The same fold as pawns with the gifts floor; no prerequisite; eligible beside pawns at the bottom of the ladder. Fixtures: five clean counted games with gifts offered [1,1,1,1,1] taken the same -> off at the sixth; offered [0,0,0,0,0] -> asking; one verified gift walked past at off gives exactly one free record, quiet woken, not asking.

**Prerequisites.** none

**How it is practised.** This look owns the opponent's notch: plan.giftRate is 0.12 while free is at asking or quiet and 0.08 once it is off; the clean-game step-down in storeGame goes away and holdon.v1.giftRate is read once as a fallback and never written. While current: the opening line 'Something of theirs may be free today. Look before you plan.'; the remembering question 'Hold on. Something of theirs is free again. Tap it.'; the K2 prompt leans toward what_is_free; a free piece walked past this game (anyway or landed, netLoss >= 2) is offered as 'Play it again from here' on the close card. At off the walked-past piece lands silently and the close caption (COPY.CLOSE.FREE_STAYED, copy-checked) reads 'A free knight was there: it stayed.'

**Retire or decay.** As pawns; a woken free returns the notch to 0.12 for the next game, which is the decay he feels: the opponent starts leaving pieces again.

### `guards`: I notice what my move leaves behind.

The two-piece look: the piece I move was guarding something, or standing in a line, and after it moves something else of mine falls. ignored_attack with sub 'lost_guard' or 'opened_line'. Rarer and subtler, so it sits later and climbs on a longer history. positions.json records opened_line (italian_nxe4 Nc3) and no lost_guard, so the lost_guard branch of nodeOf is covered by a synthetic record only.

**Evidence.**
- Against: HoldRecords with category ignored_attack and sub 'lost_guard' or 'opened_line', any outcome including landed, weighted as pawns.
- For: a counted game with zero records of this kind.
- Floor: the digest holds at least PATH.GUARDS_HISTORY (5) counted games in all; and, as every look, off needs one record ever, so a guards look that has never fired caps at quiet and is never 'yours'.

**Mastery rule.** The same fold as pawns with the history floor, climbing only while their_last_move is at quiet or off. Fixtures: six clean counted games with no guards record ever -> quiet, not off, no OFF transition, and not_yet on the face until three counted games exist; seven counted games with one guards record in the first and six clean after -> off.

**Prerequisites.** their_last_move

**How it is practised.** While current: the opening line 'When a piece moves, what was it guarding?'; the remembering questions 'Hold on. The piece you are moving was guarding something. Tap it.' and 'Hold on. Your move opens a line again. Tap along it.'; 'Play it again from here' is the main practice. No opponent change. Quiet and off as pawns, for these two sub-cases only.

**Retire or decay.** As pawns, with the decay announced in the next opening line ('A guard went missing last game. I will ask about those again.').

### `mates`: I see the mate next move, theirs and mine, and I never stalemate a won game.

Their check that mates, my mate on the board, and the stalemate that throws a win away: allowed_mate, missed_mate, allowed_stalemate. ALWAYS_HOLD; the hand never withdraws; the look lives on the ladder so the face can say so, and its state is words only.

**Evidence.**
- Against: HoldRecords with category allowed_mate, missed_mate or allowed_stalemate, any outcome, in any non-again game (a four-move Scholar's loss counts).
- Window: the last PATH.MATE_WINDOW (5) counted games for the 'quiet' word; the newest stored game of any kind for 'back'. A mate-win is not evidence (it says nothing about their check being seen).

**Mastery rule.** Words only: 'back' when the newest stored game holds a record; 'quiet' when the last five counted games hold zero records and a record exists earlier in the digest; else 'asking'. plan.hand always carries 'full' for all three categories; a unit test asserts the hand never excludes them whatever the log, and that a Scholar's mate loss (observed 3) sets 'back'.

**Prerequisites.** none

**How it is practised.** The hold itself, every time, with its full question in every state; never current; after a mate-loss the next opening line is 'When their queen is near your king, their check first.'; the face reads 'Always. This one I never let go.' and, when words-quiet, 'Always. Quiet for five games.' 'Play it again from here' is offered for a missed_mate or allowed_stalemate played anyway. The mate gift and the set finishing positions are on the later list.

**Retire or decay.** Any record returns the words to 'back' at once. Nothing on the board changes, because nothing on the board ever faded.

## Felt in the game
