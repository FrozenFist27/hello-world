# Answer the three placement positions, then start lesson 1

[Open lesson 1: Look both ways, the check before every move](lessons/0001-blunder-check.html)

The placement (`lessons/0000-placement.html`) is three positions and no score; your three answers,
moves and reasons, tell the coach where the lessons start. Lesson 1 is where they start for
everyone under 1000: games at that level are decided by pieces left for free, and the check before
every move is the habit that stops it. Thirty minutes covers both; then two slow games against the
bot this week, saying the three questions aloud before each move.
