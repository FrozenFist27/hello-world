# Chess (under 1000) Resources

## Tier 1 — Canonical (Knowledge)

- [Book: _Bobby Fischer Teaches Chess_ — Fischer, Margulies & Mosenfelder]  ·  not owned (Bantam, in print: any bookshop or public library)
  Programmed learning of checks and mating patterns, one position per page with the answer hidden until you turn it. Use for: pattern 2 (the basic mates), pattern 4 (mate-in-one threats), and the "what checks are there" question of the blunder check.
- [Book: _Learning Chess, Step 1 and Step 2_ (the Steps Method / Stappenmethode) — Brunia & van Wijgerden](https://www.stappenmethode.nl/en/)  ·  not owned (publisher's site sells the manuals and workbooks; the Step 1 trainers' manual is published free as a PDF on the same site)
  The Dutch school curriculum. Step 1 is the rules plus attack, capture and defence; Step 2 is double attack, mate in one and the basic mates. Use for: pattern 1 (attack and defence, hanging pieces), pattern 2 (queen and rook mates), pattern 4 (one-move threats). The workbooks are the exercise bank wherever tintins has no puzzle for the idea.
- [Book: _Logical Chess: Move by Move_ — Irving Chernev]  ·  not owned (Batsford, algebraic edition, in print; widely held by libraries)
  Thirty-three games with every single move explained in words. Use for: pattern 3 (opening principles shown as a plan inside whole games; the first games are 1.e4 e5) and for showing why a developing move beats a pawn grab.
- [Lichess Learn](https://lichess.org/learn) and [Lichess Practice](https://lichess.org/practice)  ·  not owned (free; Learn needs no account, Practice saves progress with a free account)
  Interactive drills: piece movement, "Protection", "Capture", checkmate patterns, and the queen and rook mates played out against the computer. Use for: pattern 1 reps between lessons, pattern 2 mates practised before the project.

## Tier 2 — Orientation

- [Video series: _Chess Fundamentals_ #1 to #5 — IM John Bartholomew (YouTube, free)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ao9iOeK_jvU)
  #1 Undefended Pieces is pattern 1 in twenty minutes from a titled player; #3 Typical Mistakes covers most of pattern 3. Watch before the lesson, never instead of it.
- [Lichess Practice](https://lichess.org/practice)
  Guided exercises on the mates and the simplest tactics; good framing before pattern 2. Not the basis of a lesson on its own.

## Tier 3 — Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/chessbeginners](https://www.reddit.com/r/chessbeginners/)
  Beginner-only and moderated, with game-review threads. Use for: "is this normal at my level?" and for seeing other people's hung pieces.
- [Lichess forum, General Chess Discussion](https://lichess.org/forum/general-chess-discussion)
  Polite and active. Use for: book and resource questions, never for an explanation.
- [r/chess](https://www.reddit.com/r/chess/)
  Larger and noisier; its wiki's beginner list is sound. Use for: a sanity check on a resource, nothing more.
