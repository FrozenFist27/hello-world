The three questions lesson one teaches

  1. What did their last move attack?
  2. What can I take?
  3. What checks are there, theirs and mine?

How answering works on this page. Each lesson asks you to explain your move in your own words. That grading runs on a small server on your computer, so here every question switches to its multiple-choice version. Pick an answer, read the feedback, and the next section opens.

To answer in your own words and have each answer graded, run the study on your computer: cd chess-study && make local, then open http://localhost:9990.
